Address

The address your phone opens, from calendar entries, pushes and the widget. It has to work when you are not at home.

{# Browsers refuse a service worker anywhere but https and localhost, and refuse it silently, so an http address means the board never opens without a connection and nothing anywhere says why. #}
The board will not open offline

You opened this over http://, and browsers only run a service worker on https://, so nothing is kept for when there is no connection. Serve it over https:// and it works on its own.

Import flag

Import flag colour
{% for colour in flag_colours %} {% endfor %}

Give a booking email this colour of flag and the flight appears on the board. Keep the colour for this and nothing else.

Why not red?

Red is the one colour Apple sends with no marker at all, so it cannot be told apart from an ordinary flag. Apple also documents that iOS Mail will not flag anything in the Promotions, Updates or Transactions categories, and airline confirmations are Transactions; List View has no such restriction.
