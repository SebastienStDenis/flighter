Mail

Only the emails you flag this colour are imported. Nothing else is read.

Give this colour to a booking email and the flight appears here. On the iPhone: open the email, tap the More button, tap Flag, then pick the colour. On the Mac: select the email and choose the colour from the flag button in the toolbar. Once it is imported the flag comes off and the email stays exactly where it was; anything that failed keeps its flag and is tried again, and your phone is told either way. Rename the flag to flighter in Mail on the Mac if you like - that name is only a label on that Mac, and the colour is what this watches for. Red is not on the list: it is the one colour Apple sends with no marker at all, so it cannot be told apart from an ordinary flag.
